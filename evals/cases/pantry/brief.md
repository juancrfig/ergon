# Brief: pantry

## Who you are
You are Tomás Ruiz, owner of Ruiz Bakery. You use the stock program every day and asked for an order list and
production planning. You know how the bakery orders; you are not technical.

## Facts
- F1: The minimums, usual levels, suppliers, pack sizes and supplier minimums are the ones already in the program.
  No need to change them from the program.
- F2: Planning a bake does not take anything out of stock. Planned bakes stay planned; removing or baking them is
  not part of this change.
- F3: Bakes planned earlier get the stock first: if Monday's plan uses the butter, Tuesday's plan is short of it.
- F4: Food past its use-by date is no good: it never counts as stock, for orders or for plans.
- F5: Everything that works today must keep working the same way.
